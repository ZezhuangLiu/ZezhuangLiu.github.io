Industrial ecology for urban metabolism, climate resilience, and low-carbon built environments.

I am a master's candidate in Environmental Science and Engineering at Chongqing University. My work integrates material flow analysis, spatial life-cycle assessment, geospatial data science, and machine learning to quantify how cities accumulate materials, intensify or mitigate urban heat islands, generate emissions, and respond to climate and disaster risks.

Research Focus

My research centers on industrial ecology methods for quantifying the physical basis of urban development: built-environment material stocks, dynamic MFA, spatial LCA, circular economy strategies, urban heat island mechanisms, carbon mitigation, climate resilience, and resilient infrastructure systems.

  • Urban metabolism and high-resolution material stock accounting
  • Urban heat islands, 2D/3D urbanization, and climate-resilient city design
  • Spatial LCA, carbon drivers, circular economy pathways, and resilient infrastructure
